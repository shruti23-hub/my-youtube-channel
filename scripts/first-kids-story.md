Lesson: Sharing and kindness
Scene 1 (0–5 seconds)
Narrator: “Once upon a time, there was a little cloud named Coco.”
Scene 2 (5–12 seconds)
Narrator: “One sunny day, Coco saw a tiny flower feeling thirsty.”
Scene 3 (12–20 seconds)
Narrator: “Coco shared a few raindrops, and the little flower began to smile!”
Scene 4 (20–26 seconds)
Narrator: “The flower bloomed beautifully. Coco felt happy too!”
Scene 5 (26–30 seconds)
Narrator: “Remember, little friends: sharing makes the world brighter!”
